What to buy: recovered leaders

As of the 2026-10-09 close. S&P 500 and 400.
Buy stocks that crashed in the last 6 months, climbed back steadily for 3 months, and now lead. About 1 in 3 of these trades finishes the next month in the top 20% of all stocks for gain over SPY relative to the worst dip along the way. A typical stock does that 1 time in 5. A portfolio of them beat SPY on return for the pain in every period tested.

What to look for

  1. It crashed. At some point in the last 6 months it fell 29% or more from a high. That puts it among the deepest 20% of drops in the market.
  2. It climbed back steadily. For the last 3 months it has stayed close to its rising high, on average within 2.5%. The typical one is now within 3% of its 52-week high.
  3. It leads, any one of: beating its sector by 18% or more over 6 months; relative strength vs SPY within 9% of its 1-year high; or rising 1.4x the market on up days.
  4. Not energy, materials or utilities.
  5. It works best when SPY is within 5% of its high: 33% hit rate there, 27% when SPY is further down.
